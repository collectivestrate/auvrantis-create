# Auvrantis Everyday Workflows

## Product direction

Auvrantis Create should be the daily workspace for making and maintaining a small-business website, not only a one-time page generator. Every feature stays editable, previewable, and reversible.

## Release sequence

1. **Design system:** saved palettes, type scale, button styles, spacing, radii, shadows, responsive overrides, and reusable section presets.
2. **Content collections:** services, posts, FAQs, testimonials, team, gallery, events, and pricing records with add/edit/reorder/archive controls.
3. **Business actions:** forms, booking links, newsletter capture, maps, downloads, and social links with explicit destination previews.
4. **Daily command center:** tasks, reminders, readiness milestones, change history, rollback, and “what changed” summaries.
5. **Collaboration:** preview links, reviewer comments, approvals, and structured Create↔Check handoffs.
6. **Check depth:** broken links, image alt coverage, form checks, mobile warnings, and evidence-backed before/after comparisons.

## Safety and product constraints

- All content and style changes preview before applying.
- Undo and local backup remain available for every change.
- AI suggestions remain structured and never execute arbitrary code.
- Forms and integrations never transmit data until the owner explicitly configures and approves a destination.
- Customer sites never receive an Auvrantis watermark; product attribution stays in the builder and tester UI.
